# Tenant Safety Guidelines — Rental Peshawar

Student and family safety is paramount. When looking for accommodation in Peshawar, adhere to the following safety protocols:

1. **Verify Landlord Badges:**
   - Always look for the **Verified Landlord Badge (✓ CNIC Verified)** on listing cards before arranging an in-person visit.
2. **Scheduled Day Visits:**
   - Use the in-app Visit Scheduling tool to coordinate visits during daylight hours.
   - For student hostels and portions (e.g. in University Town or Board Bazaar), visit with a friend, parent, or fellow student.
3. **No Upfront Cash Prior to Written Agreement:**
   - Never transfer money via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or bank transfer prior to physically inspecting the property, verifying the landlord's original CNIC, and signing a written tenancy contract.
4. **Report Suspicious Listings:**
   - If a landlord demands non-refundable viewing fees, provides incorrect location details, or exhibits harassment, immediately tap **Report Listing** or submit an Urgent ticket via Support.
5. **Keep Communications In-App:**
   - Initial inquiries, negotiation notes, and visit appointments should remain in the platform chat to ensure end-to-end auditability in case of disputes.
