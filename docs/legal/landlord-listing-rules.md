# Landlord Listing Rules — Rental Peshawar

To maintain quality and trust across Peshawar's rental ecosystem, all landlords and hostel managers must strictly observe the following rules:

1. **Mandatory Identity Verification:**
   - Listings can only be submitted for public review after uploading a clear photo of your NADRA CNIC (front and back) and verifying your Pakistani mobile phone number via OTP.
2. **Accurate Categorization:**
   - You must unambiguously choose either `STUDENTS_ONLY` or `FAMILIES_ONLY` during property creation. Mixed or dual-use listings are prohibited.
3. **Peshawar Geolocation Accuracy:**
   - The selected map pin must accurately correspond to the physical entrance of the rental portion, hostel, or house.
4. **Photo Standards:**
   - Minimum 3 distinct photographs showing the bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen/entrance.
   - Stock photos, watermarked internet images, or photos belonging to other properties will cause immediate listing rejection.
5. **Transparency on Utility Loads & UPS/Solar:**
   - Clearly state whether backup power (UPS, solar system, generator), water bore, and Sui Gas lines are provided.
6. **Availability Upkeep:**
   - When a property is rented or undergoing maintenance, update the listing status immediately to `RENTED` or `UNAVAILABLE` to avoid inquiry penalties and maintain response badges.
7. **Zero Duplicate Policy:**
   - Publishing identical listings across different titles to crowd search results will flag your account for duplicate-listing penalties.
