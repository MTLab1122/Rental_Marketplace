# Security Incident Communication Plan & Response Templates

## 1. Communication Governance & Approval Flow

During a security incident or infrastructure disruption, clear, transparent, and accurate communication prevents misinformation, protects brand integrity, and fulfills legal compliance obligations.

1. **Sole Spokesperson Principle**: No employee may communicate externally (social media, press, direct messages) regarding an ongoing incident without explicit authorization from the Communications Lead and Legal Counsel.
2. **Approval Hierarchy**:
   - `SEV1` Communications: Requires approval from CEO, CISO, and Legal Counsel.
   - `SEV2` Communications: Requires approval from Incident Commander and Communications Lead.
   - `SEV3/SEV4`: Standardized status page templates published by Support Lead.

---

## 2. Standardized Communication Templates

### Template A: Platform Outage / Unscheduled Degradation

> **Subject**: [Service Advisory] Rental Peshawar System Performance Update
>
> We are currently investigating an issue impacting [Property Search / Messaging / Mobile App Login] on Rental Peshawar.
>
> - **Current Status**: Our engineering team has identified the root cause and is actively deploying a fix.
> - **Customer Impact**: Existing inquiries and confirmed bookings remain safe and unaffected.
> - **Next Update**: Within 30 minutes at https://status.rentalpeshawar.pk.
>
> We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience.

---

### Template B: Security Incident / Account Protection Alert

> **Subject**: [Action Required] Important Security Notice Regarding Your Rental Peshawar Account
>
> As part of our continuous security monitoring, we detected suspicious login activity that may affect a small subset of accounts, including yours.
>
> - **Actions Taken**: As a precautionary measure, we have invalidated all active sessions and temporarily locked your account.
> - **Next Steps**: Please reset your password immediately using the secure link: https://rentalpeshawar.pk/reset-password.
> - **Assurance**: Your financial details and payment ledgers remain completely safe and uncompromised.
>
> If you did not initiate this request, please contact support immediately at security@rentalpeshawar.pk.

---

### Template C: Payment & Escrow Disruption

> **Subject**: [Payment Update] Temporary Processing Delay for Rental Deposits
>
> We are experiencing a temporary processing disruption with our payment gateway partner.
>
> - **Status**: Security deposits and booking payouts are temporarily paused to ensure absolute transaction safety.
> - **Funds Safety**: No double charges have occurred. Any pending intents will either be confirmed cleanly or automatically released back to your account.
> - **Resolution**: Normal processing will resume within [Estimated Time].

---

### Template D: Scheduled Maintenance Window

> **Subject**: [Notice] Scheduled Infrastructure Maintenance on [Date/Time]
>
> To improve platform security and scalability, Rental Peshawar will undergo scheduled maintenance on **[Date] from [Start Time] to [End Time] PKT**.
>
> - **Impact**: The web portal, admin dashboard, and mobile APIs will be unavailable for approximately [Duration].
> - **Assurance**: All active listings, saved searches, and tenant agreements will be preserved.
