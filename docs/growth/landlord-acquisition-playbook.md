# Landlord Acquisition Playbook - Peshawar

## 1. Funnel Architecture & Lifecycle Stages

```
[ Visitor ]
    │ (Traffic from Ads, SEO, Local Word-of-Mouth)
    ▼
[ Inbound Lead ]
    │ (Form submit, WhatsApp inquiry, Callback request)
    ▼
[ Contacted ]
    │ (< 15-minute response SLA via Phone / WhatsApp)
    ▼
[ Qualified ]
    │ (Confirmed ownership in Peshawar, vacancy within 45 days)
    ▼
[ Verified ]
    │ (CNIC + Title document / Utility bill verification)
    ▼
[ First Listing Created ]
    │ (Assisted photography, AI listing assistant coaching)
    ▼
[ First Listing Approved ]
    │ (Moderation SLA < 4 hours)
    ▼
[ First Qualified Inquiry ]
    │ (Tenant lead routed via verified messaging)
    ▼
[ Active Landlord ]
    │ (Maintains response rate > 80%)
    ▼
[ Retained & Subscribed ]
    │ (Upgrades to Pro/Agency plan for multiple units)
```

## 2. Inbound Capture Tactics

1. **Public Landlord Landing Page (`/for-landlords`)**:
   - Clean calculator estimating monthly rental income in Hayatabad vs University Town vs Warsak Road.
   - Lead capture form requiring Name, Phone Number, Locality, and Property Type.
2. **WhatsApp Direct Concierge**:
   - Click-to-chat button pre-filling: _"Salam RentPeshawar team, I want to list my property in [Locality]. Please guide me."_
3. **Internal Operator Onboarding**:
   - Field operations team visits University Town hostel managers and offers free professional photography and digital inventory creation.

## 3. First-Listing Incentive & Listing Coaching

- **Introductory Reward**: First listing receives 14 days of free **Featured Placement** upon completing identity verification.
- **Listing Quality Coaching**:
  - Automated WhatsApp prompt if photos are blurry or under 4 images.
  - Suggesting clear utility breakdown (separate PESCO meter vs shared bill) to increase inquiry rate by 3x.

## 4. Landlord Reactivation Workflows

- **Day 14 No Inquiry**: Trigger automated listing review to recommend competitive rent calibration.
- **Day 30 Inactive**: Automated check-in: _"Is your property in [Locality] still available for rent? Click to confirm or mark as rented."_
- **Rented Outside Platform**: Landlord can mark listing rented with one click, pausing inquiries and preserving reputation score.
