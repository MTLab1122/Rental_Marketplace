# Pricing & Billing Policy

## 1. Currency & Invoicing Standards

1. **Denomination**: All prices, fees, and transactions are strictly denominated in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).
2. **Tax Compliance**: Invoices explicitly detail applicable Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority (KPRA) sales tax on services where statutory thresholds apply.
3. **Sequential Invoicing**: Invoices are generated sequentially (`INV-YYYY-XXXXXX`) with immutable timestamps, line-item itemization, and tax identification numbers.

## 2. Supported Payment Workflows

Until automated production credentials are fully activated and approved with local Pakistani banking partners, the platform supports:

### 2.1 Manual Bank Transfer (Offline Verification)

- Tenant/Landlord deposits payment into designated corporate escrow bank account (e.g. Bank of Khyber / Meezan Bank).
- User uploads proof of transfer (deposit slip / digital IBFT screenshot with 6-digit RRN).
- Billing operations team verifies transaction against bank statement within 2 hours during business hours.

### 2.2 Mobile Wallets & Microfinance (JazzCash / Easypaisa)

- Direct merchant till payments or QR codes.
- Immediate transaction reference number validation.

### 2.3 International / Expatriate Cards (Stripe)

- Dedicated exclusively to overseas Pakistani property owners managing Peshawar properties from abroad.

## 3. Refund & Cancellation Terms

### 3.1 Landlord Subscriptions

- **7-Day Trial / Grace Period**: Full refund available within 7 days of initial subscription purchase if fewer than 2 listings were published.
- **Mid-Cycle Cancellation**: Immediate cancellation halts next billing cycle. Current period remains active until expiration date; no pro-rated refunds for partial months.

### 3.2 Promoted & Featured Listings

- Non-refundable once the promotion has gone live, because promotional impressions and search ranking privileges are consumed immediately.
- If a listing is rejected during post-promotion moderation due to content violations, the promotion fee is converted into platform wallet credit.

### 3.3 Digital Agreement & Escrow Fees

- Non-refundable once agreement stamping or escrow contract verification has been executed.
