## 3. Local Business vs Individual Rental Listings

In strict accordance with Google Search guidelines:

1. **LocalBusiness Schema**: Applied **only** to the official Rental Peshawar customer support hub and physical partner offices. Individual rental apartments and private houses are **never** marked up as `LocalBusiness` entities (preventing penalty for phantom Google Maps storefronts).
2. **Individual Properties**: Marked up as `Accommodation`, `Apartment`, `SingleFamilyResidence`, or `House` with associated `GeoCoordinates`.
