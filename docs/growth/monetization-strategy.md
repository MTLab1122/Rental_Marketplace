# Monetization Strategy - Peshawar Rental Marketplace

## 1. Monetization Philosophy & Principles

RentPeshawar operates on a value-accretive, multi-sided marketplace business model.

### Core Non-Negotiable Rules:

1. **Tenants Never Pay to Search or Contact**: Discovery, search, filtering, and standard messaging remain 100% free for all tenants to maximize liquidity and prevent disintermediation.
2. **No Paywalls on Basic Safety**: Tenant verification, reporting tools, and emergency contact safeguards are free features for all users.
3. **Transparent Billing**: Zero hidden fees, automatic renewals without notification, or predatory auto-debits.

## 3. Subscription Tier Architecture

### 3.1 Free Landlord Plan (PKR 0/month)

- Up to 2 active listings simultaneously.
- Standard moderation queue (24-48 hours review).
- Standard inquiry inbox with direct chat.
- Basic listing performance counts (views, inquiries).

### 3.2 Professional Landlord Plan (PKR 2,499/month or PKR 24,990/year)

- Up to 8 active listings simultaneously.
- Priority moderation queue (< 4 hours review).
- 1 Free "Featured Listing" credit every month (worth PKR 1,500).
- Advanced inquiry analytics (lead source, response time metrics).
- Landlord Verified Trust Badge on all properties.
- Pre-made message response templates.

### 3.3 Agency & Hostel Operator Plan (PKR 6,999/month or PKR 69,990/year)

- Up to 35 active listings simultaneously (or 150 individual bed units).
- Instant moderation for certified operators.
- 4 Free "Featured Listing" credits every month.
- Multi-user agent access (3 manager seats).
- CSV/Excel bulk property import tool.
- Dedicated Peshawar account manager.
