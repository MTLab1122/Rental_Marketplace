# Referral & Loyalty Program Policy

## 1. Overview & Qualifying Criteria

RentPeshawar rewards organic advocacy through two distinct referral tracks:

### 1.1 Tenant-to-Tenant Referral

- **Referral Mechanism**: Every tenant receives a unique alphanumeric code (`PESH-TENANT-XXXXXX`) and sharable deep link.
- **Qualifying Milestone**: When the referred tenant registers and submits their first accepted inquiry or booking.
- **Reward**: PKR 500 platform wallet credit (usable toward digital agreements or future booking deposits).

### 1.2 Landlord-to-Landlord Referral

- **Referral Mechanism**: Landlords share their custom link (`PESH-LANDLORD-XXXXXX`).
- **Qualifying Milestone**: When the invited landlord registers, completes identity verification, and publishes their first `APPROVED` listing.
- **Reward**: 1 Free **Featured Listing** credit (30-day duration, worth PKR 2,499) or PKR 1,500 bank payout.

## 2. Anti-Fraud & Abuse Safeguards

To prevent opportunistic farming, sybil attacks, and reward gaming, the platform enforces:

1. **Self-Referral Prohibition**:
   - Referring account and referred account cannot share identical phone numbers, email prefixes, CNIC credentials, or payout bank accounts.
2. **Device & IP Anomaly Detection**:
   - Multiple signups originating from the same device fingerprint or internal subnet within 48 hours are automatically quarantined for manual review.
3. **Disposable Email & VoIP Phone Filter**:
   - Accounts registered using temporary email providers or non-Pakistani virtual numbers are disqualified from claiming referral rewards.
4. **Reward Caps & Velocity Limits**:
   - Maximum 5 qualifying rewards per user per calendar month without verified commercial agent status.
5. **Clawback Policy**:
   - If a referred property is removed for fraud, duplicate imagery, or community guideline violations within 30 days, the associated reward is clawed back.
