## 3. Dynamic Sitemap Verification & Guardrails

The dynamic XML sitemap is generated by `GrowthService.generateSitemapXml()` with programmatic filtering:

```xml
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<urlset xmlns="http://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9">
  <url>
    <loc>https://rentalpeshawar.pk/</loc>
    <lastmod>2026-11-20</lastmod>
    <changefreq>hourly</changefreq>
    <priority>1.0</priority>
  </url>
  <url>
    <loc>https://rentalpeshawar.pk/student-rentals</loc>
    <lastmod>2026-11-20</lastmod>
    <changefreq>daily</changefreq>
    <priority>0.95</priority>
  </url>
  <url>
    <loc>https://rentalpeshawar.pk/family-rentals</loc>
    <lastmod>2026-11-20</lastmod>
    <changefreq>daily</changefreq>
    <priority>0.95</priority>
  </url>
  <!-- 15 Verified Locality Hubs -->
  <!-- Active & Approved Properties (Excludes Draft, Pending, Rented, Suspended) -->
</urlset>
```

### Sitemap Indexing Invariants

1. **Unapproved Property Exclusion**: Any property in `DRAFT`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `REJECTED`, or `SUSPENDED` status is blocked from `sitemap.xml`.
2. **Rented Out Exclusion**: Any property with `isAvailable: false` is immediately removed from the active sitemap.
3. **Thin Content Safeguard**: Localities with fewer than 1 active property trigger an administrative warning and are marked with `priority=0.5`.
